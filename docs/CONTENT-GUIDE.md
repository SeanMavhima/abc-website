# ABC website: content guide for administrators

This guide explains how to update the ABC website. You never need to write code, HTML or JSON. Everything is done through the content manager.

## Signing in

1. Go to **your-website-address/admin/login**.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Forgotten your password? Choose **Forgot password?** and follow the link in the email.

To change your name or password later, open **My account** in the sidebar.

## The basics

- **Save often.** The bar at the bottom of each editor shows **Unsaved changes** (amber) or **All changes saved** (green). If you try to leave with unsaved changes, your browser will warn you.
- **Changes are live immediately.** When you save, the public website updates within seconds.
- **Drafts stay private.** Pages and stories marked **Draft** are never shown to visitors.
- **Arrows reorder things.** Wherever you see up and down arrows, they change the order shown on the website.
- **The eye icon shows or hides.** Hidden items are kept, just not displayed.

## Editing the homepage

Open **Homepage**. The homepage is a list of sections, from top to bottom.

- **Click a section** to open it and edit its text, photographs and buttons.
- **Eye icon:** hide or show the section.
- **Arrows:** move the section up or down.
- **Copy icon:** duplicate the section.
- **Bin icon:** remove the section. It is only gone for good once you save.
- **Add section** (or **Insert here** between sections): choose a new section type.

### The hero (top of the homepage)

In the **Hero** section you can change:

- the small label, headline and supporting text
- the two buttons (text and destination)
- **Display mode:** animated slideshow, or a single static photograph
- **Seconds per slide** (4 to 20)
- **Slides:** add photographs, change their order with the arrows, or switch individual slides off

Visitors can pause the slideshow, and it pauses automatically for anyone who has asked their device or the website to reduce motion.

### Sections that use other managers

Some homepage sections take their content from elsewhere. In those sections you only edit the heading and introduction:

| Section | Content comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Programmes | **Programmes** |
| Impact statistics | **Impact statistics** |
| Stories | **Stories** (latest, or featured only) |
| Organisations | **Organisations** (choose which relationship type to show) |
| Team | **Team** |

A section with nothing to show (for example Team, before anyone is added) is hidden automatically.

## Pages

Open **Pages** to edit About, Our Work, Nature Labs, Programmes, Stories & Insights, Get Involved, Contact and Privacy Policy, or to create new pages.

Each page has:

- a **page header** (title, small label, introduction and header photograph)
- **sections**, edited exactly like the homepage
- **Publishing:** Draft or Published, and the web address
- **Search and sharing:** optional title and description for Google and social media

Core pages cannot be deleted (unpublish them instead) and keep their web addresses. New pages can use any address that isn't already taken.

**Linking to a section.** Give a section an **anchor** (for example `apply`), then link to `/get-involved#apply`.

## Programmes

Open **Programmes** to add or edit programme areas.

- **Status:** Proposed programme area, Pilot phase, Active programme or Completed. Only choose Active or Completed when that is verifiably true.
- **Published** controls visibility; **Featured** controls which programmes appear in sections set to "featured only".
- The **detailed description** uses the text editor (headings, lists, links and images).

## Stories

Open **Stories** to write articles, research updates, field notes, photo stories, videos, community stories and announcements.

1. Choose **New story**.
2. Add a title, short excerpt and the story itself.
3. Choose a **featured photograph** and a **category**.
4. Optional: add a **photo gallery** or a **video** (paste a YouTube or Vimeo link, or choose an uploaded video).
5. Set **Status** to Published, or use **Save and publish**.

**Scheduling:** set a future **publication date**. The story appears automatically at that time.

The star icon marks a story as **featured**. Categories are managed under **Story categories**.

## Team

Open **Team** and choose **Add team member**. New profiles are **hidden** until you switch them on. Please add real, confirmed team members only, with their permission to publish their name, role, photograph and biography.

## Organisations

Open **Organisations**. Each organisation has a **relationship**:

- **Prospective collaborator:** identified for possible future engagement, with no formal relationship
- **Confirmed partner:** a formal partnership exists
- **Supporting organisation:** supports ABC in another way

Important:

- Only mark an organisation as a **confirmed partner** once a relationship has actually been established.
- Only upload an organisation's **logo with its permission**.
- The homepage **Organisations** section shows confirmed partners only, so it stays hidden until you have one. To show prospective collaborators, add an Organisations section set to "Prospective collaborators". It automatically includes a note saying no formal partnership exists.

## Impact statistics

Open **Impact statistics** to add, edit, reorder, hide or delete figures. Each has a number, an optional prefix or suffix (for example `+` or `%`), a label, a short description, an icon and a **status**:

- **Target:** a planned figure (the current pilot figures are targets)
- **Achieved:** a verified result
- **Ongoing:** in progress

Every statistics section also has a **disclosure note** (edit it in the Homepage or page editor) so visitors understand what the figures mean. Replace targets with verified results as the work progresses.

## Media library

Open **Media library** to upload and manage photographs and videos.

- **Upload:** drag files onto the upload area, or choose **Choose files**. Type a **folder** name first to keep things organised.
- **Limits:** images up to 10 MB (JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, SVG); videos up to 50 MB (MP4, WebM). For longer videos, upload to YouTube or Vimeo and paste the link into a story.
- **Click any file** to edit:
  - **Alternative text:** a short description of the image for people using screen readers (required for accessibility)
  - **Caption**
  - **Credit**, for example "Photographer name, CC BY 4.0". Credited images appear on the public Image credits page.
  - **Source / licence link**
  - **Folder**
- **Where this is used** shows which pages, stories or settings use the file. Delete only files that aren't used, or remove them from that content first.

When choosing an image anywhere in the editor, you can pick from the library or upload a new one on the spot. Images marked **No alt text** still need a description.

**Photographs of people:** obtain consent before publishing, and parental consent for anyone under 18.

## Navigation and footer

Open **Navigation & footer**.

- **Main menu:** top-level items appear in the header. Add **dropdown links** beneath an item to create a menu.
- **Footer links:** each top-level item is a **column heading** with its links beneath.
- Destinations can be a page path such as `/about`, an anchor such as `/get-involved#apply`, or a full web address. Tick **New tab** for external sites.

The header button ("Get Involved"), footer description, copyright text, newsletter text, contact details and social links are in **Website settings**.

## Website settings

Open **Website settings** to change:

- organisation name, short name, tagline and description
- **logo** and **favicon** (optional; without them the built-in ABC mark is used)
- **brand colours** (use **Restore ABC colours** to undo experiments)
- default search title, description and sharing image
- **contact details:** email, phone, address and a location note. Only publish confirmed details; empty fields are hidden on the website.
- social media links
- the header button
- footer description, copyright text (`{year}` inserts the current year) and newsletter sign-up

## Messages and applications

- **Contact enquiries:** messages from the contact form
- **Applications:** volunteer, youth researcher, partnership and collaboration expressions of interest
- **Newsletter:** sign-ups from the footer

For each submission you can read the details (click it), change its status (**new → reviewed → resolved**), reply by email or delete it. **Export CSV** downloads the list (respecting the current status filter).

These records contain personal information. Keep exports secure, and delete records once they are no longer needed. When someone asks to leave the newsletter, mark them **unsubscribed** or delete them.

## Administrators

Open **Administrators** to see who can manage the website, add an administrator (with a temporary password they should change) or remove someone's access. You cannot remove yourself, and at least one administrator must remain.

## The 3D opening on the homepage

Go to **Homepage** and open the **Hero** section.

- **Opening experience:** choose the interactive 3D landscape, or photographs only. Visitors whose device cannot show 3D always see the photographs, so keep at least one good photograph in the list.
- **3D visual theme:** dawn, day, dusk, or follow the visitor's own time of day.
- **Offer ambient sound:** adds a sound button. Sound never plays by itself.
- **3D chapters:** the short messages that appear one after another as visitors scroll. Three works best. Keep each to one sentence.
- **3D information points:** markers in the landscape. Give each a title, a sentence or two, where it sits (forest canopy, hills, wetland, open water or sky) and, if you like, a link.

You cannot change the shape of the landscape itself from here.

## Biodiversity Explorer

**Atlas habitats** are the five places on the cross-section drawing. Each has a position on the drawing, a height, a description, a list of how it works (one line each) and a photograph.

**Species** are the entries visitors browse. For each one:

1. Enter the common name, and the scientific name only if you have checked it.
2. Choose the **type of entry**. Use *Educational reference* unless ABC participants actually recorded the species. This label is shown to visitors.
3. Choose its group and its main habitat. The habitat links it to the atlas.
4. Add a photograph that you are allowed to use, and fill in its credit in the Media library.
5. Check the **IUCN Red List category** at iucnredlist.org. If unsure, leave it as *Not stated*.
6. Add at least one source.
7. Set **Visibility** to Published when it is ready.

Never write where a threatened species can be found.

## Nature Labs, citizen science and research

- **Nature Labs:** add a site only once it has been agreed with the community. Keep the region general.
- **Citizen science** and **Research projects:** each has a question, a method (one step per line), who takes part, a status and findings. Leave **Findings** empty until there are checked results: the page then tells visitors that there are none yet.
- Keep statuses honest. *Proposed* and *Planned* mean nothing has started.

## Impact statistics and milestones

Each figure has a status that visitors can see:

| Status | Use it when | How it looks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed target | It is a goal | Outlined number |
| Ongoing | Work is under way and the figure may change | Solid number |
| Achieved | The result has been recorded | Solid number |
| Verified | An achieved result has been checked against records or by someone independent | Solid number |

For anything other than a target, add the **date the figure refers to** and a short **source**. Add a **goal** if you want a progress bar.

**Milestones** are the stages on the timeline. Mark one *In progress* or *Done* only when that is true.

## Community observations

Submissions are **closed** until you switch them on in **Website settings → Community observations**. Do that only when someone is ready to review them regularly.

Each submission arrives in **Observations** as *Waiting for review*. Before approving one:

1. Check the identification, and correct the name if needed.
2. Remove anything in the notes or area that pinpoints a sensitive species or someone's home.
3. Confirm that consent was given.

Approved observations appear on the website without contact details. The names of people under 18 are never shown.

## Activity history

**Activity history** lists the latest 200 changes: what was created, changed or deleted, by whom and when. It is recorded automatically and cannot be edited.
